I live around Bondi, one of Sydney's most iconic spots. 

My home sits on a hill, a little higher than the surrounding neighbourhood. If I stand at just the right spot and look down the street, I can catch a glimpse of the ocean at the edge of my view.  Such a great place to write.

I've been thinking about writing for years, but I was never really sure what I wanted to say.

It'll probably be a lot about ML and software. I've spent too much time on them, and I do love them so much. In 2017, I taught myself to code and managed to break into the software industry. I've been here ever since. The industry has been changing quite a lot lately, and I'd like to jot down some notes along the way. Mostly to organise my own thoughts. I hope you'll find something useful here too.
